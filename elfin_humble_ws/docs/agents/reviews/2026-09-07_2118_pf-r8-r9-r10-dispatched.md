## What the consensus changed

Three rounds. The plan's two evidence corrections survived unchanged and were
independently confirmed against the raw data: the `top_surface_rate` failure is
a static border false positive rather than FOV clipping, and the
`active_output_hz` failure is preprocessor throughput (2517 raw images against
435 preprocessed frames) rather than downstream join loss (397/477 joins
succeed). The decomposition shape was accepted. Substantive corrections:

1. **The hold cannot bridge the observed dropouts.** Of the 42
   false-positive-only frames, 37 have no accepted detection in the prior 5
   frames, and rows 0-20 and 122-142 are contiguous 21-frame runs at 0.263 s
   median spacing against a ~0.26-0.29 s frame period. So they are real ~5 s
   dropouts. `temporal_window_frames: 5` bridges ~1.4 s. PF-R8 therefore gained
   suitcase recall as first-class scope (user decision), with a derived bar:
   max consecutive miss run <= 2, because window 5 at ratio 0.5 tolerates
   exactly `k <= 2`.
2. **My first `consensus: reached` was wrong** and has been retracted. It came
   from a third-round prompt narrowed to the two findings I had surfaced, while
   the reviewer's fuller audit in its own thread still rejected the revision. My
   thread `2026-09-07_2010_...` is `superseded`; the `2026-09-07_2026` thread is
   authoritative. The three rows were parked at `dispatch_ready: no` in the
   interim and only lifted after consensus was genuinely recorded.
3. `active_output_hz` is computed from every `DetectionFrame` stamp regardless
   of validity, so PF-R8 cannot move that bar. PF-R9 primarily owns it but does
   raise `top_surface_rate` too, since a frame with no cloud fails closed.
4. The A1 fixture could be satisfied by a pure border test, which the plan's own
   risks call unsafe; it now requires a labelled edge-clipped true positive.
5. A3 is causal-only, and the frames no causal 5-frame hold can recover are
   counted and assigned to recall rather than demanded of the gate.
6. PF-R5's 0.9606 was rejected as A4's baseline: it predates the predicate and
   does not share the numerator. A pre-change baseline must be recomputed.
7. B1 now requires the matched-header receipt-lag distribution and clock domain
   (per-topic periods alone cannot set B2's deadline); B2 states the behaviour
   when the justified deadline reaches `camera_horizon_sec`; B3/B4 share one
   120 s window with 15 s warmup and explicit denominators; B5 makes mutation
   isolation non-negotiable and gives decimation conditional geometry
   acceptance; C2 gets a least-squares RSS slope <= 2 MiB/min.
