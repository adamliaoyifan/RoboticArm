- The seed source is `src/luggage_gazebo/models/arm_realsense/arm_realsense_v1.3.stl`,

- The board must be emitted at **true scale** with a printed legend carrying
  dictionary, square length, marker length, and grid size, so the physical

intrinsics (`fx 323.18`) a 37.5 mm marker is only about 12 px at 1.0 m and
will not decode. The plan therefore specifies capture at 1280 colour, where
`fx` is about 646. This is not a parity violation because `T_flange_camera` is
a rigid-body transform and is resolution independent.
