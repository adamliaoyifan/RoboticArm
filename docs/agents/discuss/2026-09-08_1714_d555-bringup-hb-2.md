The question being answered is the one the operator could not settle by
looking: is the delivered depth product colour-aligned or depth-native? D555
uses the D450 module, so depth and colour differ in origin (about 59 mm
nominal, 95 mm baseline) **and** in field of view (depth 87x58, RGB 90x65).
That combination cannot be resolved from a datasheet, and it is the open
question `Q-20260908-3` needs answered to decide the canonical depth image for
the F3 depth-primary contract.

Method summary, with the plan as the authority:

1. High-contrast planar target with a sharp colour boundary at roughly 0.6 m,
   1.0 m, and 2.0 m. Stay at or beyond the ~26 cm Min-Z and prefer the
   0.6-6 m ideal range.
2. Project depth points into the colour image using the colour `K` and the
   `depth_to_color` measured in HB-1. Report **signed** u and v pixel offsets
   between the colour boundary and the depth discontinuity. Signed, not
   magnitude; the sign identifies which extrinsic assumption is wrong.
3. Convert each offset to metres at that depth and compare against the 95 mm
   baseline and the ~59 mm nominal colour offset. State which assumption the
   data supports.
4. State the aligned-or-native verdict from the evidence, not from the
   datasheet expectation.
5. If a coloured cloud topic exists, verify colour is actually populated rather
   than constant.

A null result is acceptable if it is quantified. "Looks right" is not a pass.
