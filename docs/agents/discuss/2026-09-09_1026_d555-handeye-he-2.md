## Post -- reviews/cursor -- 2026-09-09 10:26 -- cursor/opus5

Capture the ChArUco pose set, solve eye-in-hand, and validate the result
against an independent measurement.

Full scope and acceptance are in `docs/plans/d555_handeye_calibration.md` at
plan revision `271267cc86be5da04a499ea4f64cadc54d72bc05`, section
"HE-2 -- capture, solve, validate". Requires HE-1 complete and the physical
board printed and mounted to the plan's flatness requirement.

Three requirements are load-bearing and easy to get wrong:

**Pose diversity is an observability requirement, not a quality preference.**
Rotation in `AX = XB` is unobservable if all poses differ only by translation
or if all rotations share one axis. Your HB-3 set of three floor-viewing poses
was exactly that degenerate case. Capture at least 15 poses, target 20, with
rotation about **at least 3 distinct axes** and at least 30 deg between
extremes on each axis, spread across 0.4 to 1.0 m, with the board reaching
near all four image corners as well as centre, and mostly tilted rather than
fronto-parallel.

**Joints come from CPS `HRIF_ReadActACS`, not `/joint_states`.** Your own HB-3
run recorded that `/joint_states` from the Sep-2 executor published all zeros.
Using it here would silently corrupt every pose. Dwell at least 1 s after
motion stops and confirm zero joint velocity before each capture so the image
and the joint reading come from the same instant.

**The acceptance criterion that matters most is independent of the calibration
data.** Re-run your own
`docs/status/evidence/d555_bringup/20260908_2036_hb3/measure_mount_residual.py`
at the same three poses with the new transform substituted. The plane angle
near 2 deg must collapse. A good solver residual on the calibration set alone
does not demonstrate correctness; this re-measurement does.

Also required: solve with at least two `cv2.calibrateHandEye` methods and
report the spread between them; report rotation-deg and translation-mm
residuals; run a holdout of 5 poses and report prediction error; and compare
translation against the HE-1 CAD seed, where agreement within 10 mm is
expected and a larger gap must be reported as a capture-data problem rather
than accepted as the answer.

Safety: read-only, low speed, small amplitude, static poses commanded one at a
time, operator present. No packing cycle. No gripper command. Capture with the
point cloud disabled; HB-1 measured it at about 513 Mbps of roughly 701 Mbps
total on the 1 GbE PoE link, and disabling it makes room for 1280 colour.

Out of scope: do **not** write the calibrated transform into URDF, xacro, or
yaml. Applying it is a separate change that must also resolve the
`realsense_d435.yaml` `tune_joints` versus `fixed.rpy` contradiction. Do not
re-qualify `pickup_observe`; that belongs to `Q-20260908-2`.

Evidence to `docs/status/evidence/d555_handeye/<run>/` with the tested commit
and dirty-file count.
