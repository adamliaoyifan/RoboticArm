## Summary

Intel has no SKU named D455i; D455 already includes the BMI055 IMU. Official ROS TF origin is left IR / depth, coincident with `camera_link`. Color is nominally 59 mm along `-Y`, right IR 95 mm along `-Y`. Optical frames share those origins with `rpy(-pi/2, 0, -pi/2)`. This workspace still models a D435 `rgbd_camera` whose points live in `camera_link` axes.
