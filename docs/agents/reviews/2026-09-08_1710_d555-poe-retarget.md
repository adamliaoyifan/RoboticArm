## Summary

The user confirmed the alignment target is the installed **D555 PoE**, not
D455. That answers question 1 of `Q-20260908-2` and supplies the physical
identity that `Q-20260908-3` recorded the camera-body decision as blocked on.

Retargeting is not a rename. Verified D555 datasheet numbers contradict five
assumptions currently in this workspace, so the D455 material was demoted to
reference and the contradictions were recorded for reviews rather than
implemented.

D555 is a D450 global-shutter module with IMU on a Vision SoC V5: 95 mm
baseline, depth FOV 87 x 58, RGB FOV 90 x 65, depth to 1280x720, RGB to
1280x800, IP65, 5.5 W typical, **167 x 42 x 48 mm, 337 g**, PoE RJ45 with
SafeDDS.

Contradictions found:

1. **Mount.** The workspace asserts D555 fits the D435 90 mm pocket
   (`replacement.md` 2.1, the `d555_link` identity in
   `realsense_d435.urdf.xacro`, and `test_d555_d435_mount.py`). A 167 mm body
   cannot; it is longer than the D455 body this workspace already rejected as
   too long. The plan's reasoning inverts, because it used "D555 fits" as the
   premise for doubting D455. `camera_link` to `d555_link` identity is the
   load-bearing assumption under all hand-eye geometry and must be re-measured.
2. **Min-Z.** D555 is ~26 cm at VGA, ~52 cm at max resolution, ideal 0.6-6 m.
   The `pickup_observe` box top sits about 0.24 m away, so the pose is still not
   covered. D555 shrinks the blind-zone problem rather than removing it.
3. **Stream profile.** The 2026-09-02 snapshot measured stable operation at
   640x360 at 15 Hz and recorded that 896x504 at 30 Hz **dropped the DDS
   device**. Sim is 640x480 at 30 Hz with a 0.105-3.0 m clip. That is a
   simultaneous change of aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and near range.
4. **Transport.** D555 has no USB streaming path; it is a network DDS
   participant over PoE requiring jumbo frames and MTU 9000. No composable-node,
   intra-process, or shared-memory arrangement can remove full-cloud transport
   cost on hardware, which closes off "keep the cloud and optimise transport".
5. **Distro.** The working bring-up was native ROS 2 Jazzy with librealsense
   2.58.4, against a Humble 22.04 workspace.

Contradiction 3 is also direct hardware support for F3: raising payload
de-enumerated the camera rather than merely slowing the chain, so depth-primary
is load-bearing for sensor availability independent of the B3 bars. This was
posted to `Q-20260908-3` without contesting its sequencing, which keeps F3
ahead of the body decision.

## Risks

- Rate and window bars across PF-R8, PF-R9, and PF-R10 are expressed for
  30 Hz input. At 15 Hz the wall-clock bars need re-derivation; the B3 emission
  bar survives because it is a ratio. This may require new generations rather
  than amendments.
- If the `camera_link` to `d555_link` identity is wrong, every PF-R geometry
  measurement taken so far inherits an uncorrected mount error.
- D555 DDS support under Humble is unverified. If it requires Jazzy, the edge
  board choice is forced, which couples to Orin (JetPack 6 / 22.04 / Humble)
  against Thor (JetPack 7 / 24.04 / Jazzy).
- The hardware topic namespace is `/camera/d555/...` per the in-repo RViz
  config, not the `sensor_data_pipeline.md` `/camera/...` contract, so parity
  needs a namespace and profile layer rather than parameters alone.
- No D555 sim model exists. Nothing in this change creates one; the geometry
  gate must pass first.
